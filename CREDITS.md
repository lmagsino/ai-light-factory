# Credits

AI Light Factory was designed and written by **Leo Magsino Jr**, including the line's structure, the five rules in [docs/rules.md](docs/rules.md), the band and the guardrails. It also stands on good ideas that other people had first:

- **Addy Osmani** coined *light* and *dark* software factories in ["Software Factories, Light and Dark"](https://addyosmani.com/blog/software-factories/) (2026), including the rule that a loop gets only as much autonomy as you can cheaply and reliably verify. His [agent-skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills) pack (MIT) is the recommended craft layer inside the Developer and Reviewer seats. His [factory](https://github.com/addyosmani/factory) (MIT) is the reference issue-queue factory.
- **Matt Pocock**'s [skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) (MIT): the Architect stage borrows the interview style of `grill-with-docs`, and the ticket shape owes a lot to `to-tickets`.

No code from these projects is copied into this repository. If you contribute something that is adapted from another project, keep its copyright line and license, and add it here.
